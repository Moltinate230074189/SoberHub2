ABOUT SOBER HUB

Recovery, support, skills and opportunity in one ecosystem.

Sober Hub is a community-based recovery support service called the Katlehong Sober-Hub and Skills Lab.

MISSION

Empower the recovery journey.

To empower individuals on their recovery journey through support, education, mentorship and community.

VISION

A healthier future.

To become a trusted digital recovery platform that inspires healthier, happier and substance-free communities.

WHY CHOOSE SOBER HUB?

Affordable. Holistic. Local.

Affordability

Survey-validated pricing of R50–R100 for the Katlehong community.

Holistic support

Recovery support is combined with skills development, productive activities and employment support.

Localised technology

Uses familiar technology such as WhatsApp and gaming to make support engaging.

WHO BENEFITS?

Young people, families, businesses and the wider community.

Young people

Youth recovering from drug and substance abuse.

Families

Families affected by the negative effects of substance abuse.

Businesses

Businesses benefiting from a developing workforce.

Community

The wider community benefits from productive, supported young people.
