Sober Hub Brand Guide

A visual identity system created to communicate recovery, trust, support and personal growth consistently.

Logo

The official logo is the main visual representation of the Sober Hub brand and should remain consistent across all digital and printed communication.

Meaning and usage

The logo represents recovery, support, hope and personal transformation. The figure represents an individual moving towards a healthier future, while the surrounding hands communicate care and protection.

Maintain clear space around the logo.
Use the official colours and proportions.
Do not stretch or distort the logo.
Do not recolour or rotate the logo.

Mission and Vision

Mission

To empower individuals on their recovery journey through support, education, mentorship and community.

Vision

To become a trusted digital recovery platform that inspires healthier, happier and substance-free communities.

Buttons

Buttons use clear labels, suitable contrast and consistent spacing to make actions easy to understand.

Primary action

Used for the most important action on a page.

Secondary action

Used for an alternative or lower-priority action.

Emergency action

Reserved for urgent support and emergency features.

Iconography

Icons should be simple, recognisable and used together with text labels where clarity is important.
